# 1.1.6

Elves can finally have children for a sensible share of their long lives. The game cut every woman off at 40, elf or not, so a Fae who lives to 265 spent two centuries past her childbearing years. Each tier now has its own cutoff: Elf Blood 48, Elf 55, High Elf 65, True Elf 90, Fae 140, Fae Radiant 165, while humans keep their 40. Celestials and higher go dormant after 165 and can only conceive during a rare Fertile Season that wakes for three years every few decades. Characters past their cutoff show a "Past Childbearing" or "Dormant Fertility" status. The check runs every four years, so a cutoff can land a little late; consider it a grace period

Balance: The Rut no longer leaves a permanent fertility bonus behind. Every outbreak was quietly adding +100 fertility to every adult for life, on top of the intended three-year boost, so after a few decades Aeluran courts were multiplying with a zeal the Divine Spark never asked for. Only the three-year boost remains, so expect a baby boom per Rut rather than a permanent one

Bug Fix: The Rut's character buff now shows its name instead of a raw script key

Bug Fix: The Calaquendi estate's tax automation can be given a target satisfaction like the other estates. It was stuck at 50% and the + and - buttons did nothing

Bug Fix: The Shrine to Vána now takes 120 days to build instead of one. Divine favour is quick; masonry is not

Bug Fix: Dynastic marriages no longer fill the error log when the AI attempts one without a partner at the start of a campaign. No effect on gameplay, just fifty fewer lines of noise

Thanks to Nezerathian for all of the fixes and the fertility rework in this release
