# 1.1.4

Bug Fix: Unique elven units show their own pictures again. Update 1.4 changed how the game picks unit art (by script entries instead of texture names), so Fey Archers, Elf Noble Lancers, Ranger High Guard, Wolf Riders, the Mythril-plated Fey Vanguard and Horse Masters, the Calaquendi High Magi and the Aeluran Magi had all fallen back to generic pictures in the advances tree, recruitment lists and unit panels

Bug Fix: Goods prices and demand match update 1.4 again. The mod's copies of 34 vanilla goods (kept for Calaquendi demand) predated 1.4: gold, silver, books and elephants still sold at the old base prices, and the new climate requirements for wine, livestock, sugar, pepper and saffron, the winter, disease, regional and literacy demand modifiers, and several retuned demand values were missing. Every copied good is now the 1.4 version plus the Calaquendi line

Bug Fix: The Calaquendi estate now appears when a country adopts elven culture at the start of the game. A household of High Nobles settles in the capital and two estate characters join the court, as the elven realms get at game start; previously the estate only ever existed for countries that began the game elven

Bug Fix: Close-kin marriages work again under the Avunculate and Unrestricted Marriage policies. Update 1.4 made the game refuse any marriage between close relatives (parents and children, grandparents and grandchildren, siblings, uncles or aunts with nieces or nephews) regardless of our Consanguinity Law, so the Dynastic Marriage action took the legitimacy hit and married nobody; cousins were never affected. The action now arranges such a match through the law's dispensation (the game's own kinship check is sidestepped for that one wedding), greys out matches the game refuses for other reasons with the game's own explanation, and only charges legitimacy when the marriage actually takes place
