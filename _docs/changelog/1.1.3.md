# 1.1.3

Compatibility update for Europa Universalis V 1.4 "Río Salado". Start a new game; 1.3 saves are not worth rescuing.

The elves are back on the map. Update 1.4 moved where the game reads its starting world from, and for a while every elven realm existed only as a name with no land, no ruler and no people. They have been found and resettled.

Estates work again. The game now weighs political power per pop instead of per estate; the Calaquendi keep their outsized weight (High Nobles are still High Nobles), and the stale copy that left every estate in the world with zero power is gone.

Elves can catch smallpox again. The 1.4 portrait system added illness, scar and expression shapes to every face; our long-eared heads now carry them too, so characters no longer load with missing parts.

Bug Fix: new characters without a country (cardinals, religious order heads, expedition leaders) no longer trip the elven race check every day.

Bug Fix: The Aeluran advisor can take her cabinet seat again. 1.4 started checking who is allowed into a cabinet before the game script could seat her; she now arrives with the same cabinet right the "Grant Cabinet Right" action gives.

The Government, Lobby, Character and Military panels have been rebuilt on the 1.4 layouts (the Laws tab now lives inside Government, as in vanilla), keeping the elven race and bloodline displays, the Aeluran cabinet card and the Magi unit category.

Bug Fix: The Mercury Patio now matches the 1.4 version of the building and still works on mythril.

The Calaquendi can now raise private armies, like the Nobles can in 1.4: the Personal Retinues privilege lets the great houses keep private regiments at their own expense (same size and mix as the Nobles' retinues).

Rulers and heirs may marry Calaquendi again. 1.4 added a per-estate marriage permission and the High Nobles had been left out.

Taur Iaur finally exists. Its capital was spelled wrong since the country was written, so it never appeared; it now sits at Tayga between Windy Vale and Orofarn, and Windy Vale keeps Belya as the capital it was always meant to have.
