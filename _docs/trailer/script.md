# Elf Destiny — ModCon Trailer Script (v1)

Format: **5:00, text cards + music, pure in-game footage.** No voiceover, no AI imagery (ModCon rules).
Music structure: mysterious/ambient (Act 1–2 build) → epic (Act 3 onward), or one track with a strong build at ~1:30.

Typography: Text+ cards in **Cinzel** (free SIL OFL font) or similar engraved-fantasy serif, ivory `#EAE0C8` on black or over darkened footage, slow fade in/out (0.5s in, 0.7s out). Cards either full-screen on black between shots (Act 1, act transitions) or lower-third over footage (mid-act).

### Pacing rules
- Shots 3–8s, cut on beats. Only two long holds: the teleport (Shot 16) and the Fae ascension (Shot 21).
- Cards on screen ≥2.5s, never two cards over one shot unless the shot is ≥8s.
- Total card count ~28 — comfortable at 5:00; cut Act 5 to three cards first if running long.
