# Elf Destiny Trailer — Music Shortlist (v1)

All candidates are YouTube-monetization safe. **Pick by ear, then report your choice + source to the ModCon organisers early (their rule 2).**

**Licenses:**

## Slot 1 — Mysterious opener (Acts 1–2, ~90s)

1. ⭐ **Fantasy Medieval Mystery Ambient** — DeusLower — 3:20 — Pixabay, no attribution
   https://pixabay.com/music/mystery-fantasy-medieval-mystery-ambient-292418/
   Evolving medieval-fantasy ambient; easy to lift a clean 90s.
2. **Mists in the Elven Lands** — elias_weber — 13:47 — Pixabay
   https://pixabay.com/music/main-title-mists-in-the-elven-lands-127808/
   Long-form ethereal elven ambient; total freedom picking a passage.
3. **Magic Forest** — Kevin MacLeod — 4:20 — CC-BY
   https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN1600044
   Darker, enchanted-woods mood — fits an ominous cold open.
4. **Whisper of the Eldertrees** (female elven vocals) — kaazoom — 3:37 — Pixabay
   https://pixabay.com/music/choir-whisper-of-the-eldertrees-fantasy-elven-song-rpg-female-vocals-448091/
   Most distinctively "elven"; vocals are fine since we have no VO.

## Slot 2 — Epic build (Acts 3–6, ~3.5–4 min, needs a clean ending hit)

1. ⭐ **War Preparations – Epic Orchestral** — Cyberwave-Orchestra — 4:25 — Pixabay, no attribution
   https://pixabay.com/music/main-title-war-preparations-epic-orchestral-music-387985/
   Right length, rising martial energy for beat-synced cuts.
2. **Final Battle of the Dark Wizards** — Kevin MacLeod — 4:32 — CC-BY
   https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN1500085
   Documented structure: slow build → drums kick in → climax. **Best single-track option for the whole trailer** (quiet open covers Slot 1).
3. **Five Armies** — Kevin MacLeod — 2:33 — CC-BY
   https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN1100875
   "Big giant building piece… soaring brass ending" — short; would need Slot 1 extended.
4. **Epic Cinematic** — SoundGalleryByDmitryTaras — 3:01 — Pixabay
   https://pixabay.com/music/epic-classical-epic-cinematic-501670/
5. **Fantasy Adventure Quest** — alex-morgan — 3:29 — Pixabay
   https://pixabay.com/music/adventure-fantasy-adventure-quest-537478/
   Brighter/heroic — fits a triumphant rather than ominous finale.
