# Elf Destiny Mod Description (for the ModCon host)

## Description (read-aloud, ~2 paragraphs)

**Elf Destiny** is a fantasy overhaul for *Europa Universalis V*, a remake of the *Crusader Kings III* mod of the same name, that adds a lost elven civilization to the game's historical map. Over eighty reclusive elven counties are scattered across the world for you to play from the start, but you don't have to begin as one. You can take a normal human nation and, at some point, an archaeologist named Julia Melwood arrives at your court with a map to the tomb of the Last Elf Emperor. Fund her expedition, and what she brings back, an ancient amulet called the Sigil of the Realm Lord, can turn your whole realm into elves: your ruler, your court, and your people, along with a new culture, a new religion, and technologies the world had forgotten. The setting is original, but it borrows openly from Tolkien (mythril and lembas, Mallorn trees, the goddess Vána, the high-elven Calaquendi) and various other sources of elven myth like the Celtic Tuatha Dé Danann. Its main theme is the rebirth of a forgotten people.

What sets it apart from most fantasy conversions is that it's built on EU5's real historical map rather than a separate fantasy world, so an ordinary campaign can gradually turn into a fantasy one. On top of that it adds several systems of its own. You can restore a network of ancient Grand Portals and use them to teleport whole armies across the map, at a cost that grows with distance and army size, and it comes with its own map mode. There is a matriarchal religion, the Aeluran Order, led entirely by women (Sisters, Matrons, and Matriarchs, known to commoners as the Red Witches) who serve a god called the Divine Spark. The order is inspired directly by the Bene Gesserit of *Dune* and the Aes Sedai of *The Wheel of Time*. Elven armies field battlefield Magi supplied to them by the Aelurans. A single ruler can climb a nine-step path of Ascension, from mortal, to Elf, to Fae, to demi-gods; Each step grows the ruler's power and extends their life till they reach true immortality. 

## Factoids

- There are **81 playable elven realms** hidden on the map, and any human nation can become the eighty-second by discovering its elven past.
- The transformation is **optional and emergent**. You can turn a devout human kingdom into an immortal elven theocracy almost by accident.
- The Aeluran faith is led **only by women** (Sisters, Matrons, and Matriarchs), whom common folk fear as the Red Witches. They are inspired directly by the Bene Gesserit of *Dune* and the Aes Sedai of *The Wheel of Time*.
- The mod's elves don't just live long. At the highest tiers of Ascension they **stop aging entirely** and become genuinely immortal, able to outlast their neighbors' dynasties.
- The Grand Portal network has its **own map mode**, and teleporting an army costs more the farther and the larger the army you send.
- It draws heavily on **Tolkien** (mythril, lembas waybread, Mallorn trees, the Calaquendi), with a thread of Celtic myth in the Tuatha Dé Danann.
- Early on, the story makes you **choose whether elves and humans live as equals or whether elves rule over men**, and it shapes the rest of the campaign.
